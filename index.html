Independent Research: ColoScreen

ColoScreen - Machine Learning for Early Triage of Visible Rectal Bleeding

ColoScreen is a medical AI research project designed leveraging Generative AI (Stable Diffusion) and Bayesian inference to detect GI pathologies i.e. detect and classify blood on toilet paper using computer vision. By leveraging a hybrid pipeline of physical data acquisition, digital compositing, and generative synthetic data (500K images), the model accounts for variances in substrate texture, lighting, and blood morphology to provide accurate triage.

Current Phase: Data Engineering & Model Architecture Design

  • Engineering a generative AI pipeline (Stable Diffusion, LoRA, and Albumentations) to create 500,000+ photorealistic images utilizing AWS 8 A10G instances.
  • Standardized sensor variability using Sekonic light meter and X-Rite ColorChecker on 18% gray backgrounds to ensure dataset consistency.
  • Designing a MobileNet V3 Small machine learning architecture with a custom Bayesian CNN for uncertainty-aware triage on edge devices.
